About AstroAskAQuestion

AstroAskAQuestion is an Android application designed to make Vedic astrology guidance simple, accessible, private and easy to use.

Create one or more birth profiles, ask astrology questions using your saved birth details and receive personalized astrology guidance directly within the application. Generate basic kundli matching reports.

The application has been designed with privacy, simplicity and ease of use in mind while supporting multiple Indian languages.

How It Works

1️⃣ Create Your Profile

Enter your birth details once and securely save your profile.

2️⃣ Ask Your Question

Type your astrology question to ask it.

3️⃣ Receive Your Answer

Your astrology answer is displayed inside the application.

4️⃣ View Past Answers

Access your previous questions and answers from the history section.

5️⃣ Generate Basic Kundli Matching Reports

Enter Bride and Groom birth details and generate a Basic Kundli Matching Report.
